# Video Production Workflow — Art Direction

## Visual thesis

A production control board crossed with a transit map: editorial enough to
feel considered, operational enough that the current stage, branch, proof
gate, and next artifact can be understood in one scan.

## Reference board

The implementation borrows structural mechanics, not brand identities:

1. rail-network diagrams for one shared spine and clearly named forks;
2. film slates for numbered stages and compact production metadata;
3. post-production cue sheets for artifact-first handoffs;
4. factory status boards for automation, human, and proof-gate states;
5. museum labels for short explanatory copy beside a strong visual object;
6. newspaper production desks for dense information with calm hierarchy;
7. split-screen edit monitors for paired `9:16` and `16:9` derivatives;
8. George Showroom's finance example for local-only assets, synthetic-data
   disclosure, and restrained responsive panels.

## Tokens

- Type: system sans for narrative; system monospace for stages, states, and
  artifact names. No remote fonts.
- Canvas: `#f2eee6` warm stock.
- Ink: `#17191c`.
- Muted ink: `#66645e`.
- Rule: `#c9c3b7`.
- Agent/automatic: `#3159ff` cobalt.
- Creator decision: `#ff7043` signal orange.
- Verified/proof: `#bde93b` acid chartreuse.
- Panel: `#fffdf8`.
- Radius scale: `10 / 18 / 28px`.
- Shadow: one low, hard offset; no floating glass panels.

## Layout

- Desktop: a four-column route with one continuous top rail. Every stage owns
  one job, one status, one human-facing output, and its Phase 0–5 mapping.
- The talking-head/vlog switch changes only the branch-specific work inside
  stages two and three; the four-stage frame never moves.
- The derivative panel uses two native frames anchored to one locked content
  spine. It must never imply that landscape is a crop of vertical.
- The lower repository lane names responsibility boundaries without exposing
  local paths or creator data.
- Mobile: stages become a vertical route. The rail moves to the left edge,
  controls remain reachable, and no horizontal scrolling is allowed.

## Motion budget

1. One short route-and-card reveal on first paint.
2. One opacity/translate transition when the creator changes format branch.
3. A short state pulse only on the active creator gate.

All motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No parallax, particles,
decorative marquees, or continuous ambient animation.

## Do not add

- raw footage, real transcripts, unpublished titles, local paths, or history;
- ornamental diagrams that compete with the four-stage route;
- fake product UI, stock-photo proof, glassmorphism, or neon glow;
- more than one accent color in a single status chip;
- a fifth creator-facing stage to mirror an internal proof gate.
